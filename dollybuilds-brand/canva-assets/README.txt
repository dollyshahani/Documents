dollybuilds — Canva Brand Assets
=================================
Upload these to Canva (Brand Kit > Photos, or an "Uploads" folder). Reuse forever.

TRANSPARENT PNGs (drop onto any design):
  wordmark.png        -> Brand Kit > Logos. Your "dolly builds" wordmark.
  bloom.png           -> your signature doodle flower. ONE per practical post, in a corner.
  sparkle.png         -> honey-gold 4-point star. Dreamy posts only, max 2-3.
  sparkle_pink.png    -> rose-pink version of the same.

BACKGROUNDS (1080x1350, use as full-slide backgrounds):
  pearl-shimmer-bg.png -> dreamy/CTA slides, milestone posts, highlight covers.
  butter-glow-bg.png   -> soft/reframe slides.
