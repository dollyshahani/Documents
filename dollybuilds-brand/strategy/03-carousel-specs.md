# Carousel Build Specs (Canva)

All slides 1080×1350. Reuse the same layer language across all carousels — arch, torn paper, polaroid, washi tape, pearls, one bloom. That repetition IS the brand.

**Brand DNA to paste into any AI design prompt:**
> Palette: Paper #F7F1E3 (default bg) · Butter #F6E7A6 (dreamy bg + glow) · Ink #1B1B18 (text) · Plum #43395F (headers) · Honey #DCAF6E (sparkles, CTA) · Rose #F2A9C4 (emphasis) · Lilac #D8CCEF · Pearl shimmer #F9D8E5→#E3D9F6→#CFEAF0→#F8ECC8 (dreamy only). Type: Caveat Bold = feelings/hooks; Poppins = facts/lists. One doodle bloom per practical slide; 2–3 sparkles on dreamy slides only. Text on cream cards when over texture. 1080×1350, ≥80px margins, footer @dollybuilds.

---

## Carousel A — Burnout Reset offer (pin this)
1. **Hook** — pink ripple texture, cream arch frame, Caveat 90pt: *"If you make good money and still cry on Sundays —"* + Poppins plum: **"this is yours."** Hibiscus behind arch, 3 pearls, washi tape corner.
2. **Mirror** — cream; torn-paper sheet (-2°) with 4 lines + pink ✗: *You wake up tired. / You perform "fine" all day. / You've googled "how to start over" in incognito. / You keep saying "after this deadline."* One pearl as paperweight.
3. **Reframe** — butter + glow; Caveat 110pt plum: *"You're not lazy."* / Poppins: *"You're a racehorse being asked to pull a cart."* 2 sparkles, tiny bloom.
4. **What's inside** — sand/shells texture dimmed; two cream cards like an open journal; "7 DAYS · 10–20 MIN/DAY" + day list, each day in a honey circle; washi tape.
5. **Product** — pearl marble; Burnout Reset cover in a polaroid (-3°); Caveat: *"the plan I wish someone handed me at my desk."*
6. **Receipt** — cream; your mirror selfie in a taped polaroid; Poppins: *"BITS. MBA. Manhattan consulting salary. I still cried on Sundays. So I built the way out I couldn't find."*
7. **Objections** — butter; 3 cream sticker-pills: *"Not therapy" / "Not a 5AM routine" / "Not quitting your job tomorrow."* Caveat: *"Seven days of small, doable shifts."*
8. **CTA** — golden-hour glitter water; cream card, plum border, Caveat 90pt: *"Comment WHY"* + *"and I'll send it to you."* Pearls rising like bubbles.

## Carousel B — WHY Journal launch (tool-with-proof)
1. Cream, Caveat: *"This one page stopped my 2AM spiral."*
2. *"It's 2AM. Tomorrow needs you. Your mind is replaying that meeting on loop."*
3. SHOW the tool — recreate the "2AM Page" worksheet (5 prompts + lines) on a cream card, plum frame.
4. Butter: *"A thought that survives cross-examination deserves your attention. Most don't survive."*
5. What's inside — 6 page types listed.
6. Cover mockup on pearl shimmer, sparkles.
7. CTA: *"Comment WHY — I'll DM you the journal."*

## Carousel C — Mirror / upsell bridge (no product till the end)

2–4. One inner sentence per slide, cream + bloom, Poppins with one Caveat phrase in pink: *"I'm the strong one. Nobody checks on me." / "I know exactly what to do. I just can't make myself do it." / "If I slow down, everything falls apart. If I don't, I fall apart."*
