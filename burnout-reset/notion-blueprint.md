## Sub-page: Day 1 — Name What's Draining You

**[Callout]** 📖 Read Day 1 in your PDF first (2 minutes), then fill this in honestly. Nobody sees this but you.

**[Toggle: "The Burnout Audit"]** — inside, these prompts as headings with empty text below each:
1. What does a normal week look like for me right now — honestly?
2. Which moments of the day drain me the most?
3. What am I most tired of pretending to be fine about?
4. If I could hand one responsibility to someone else this week, it would be…
5. My energy today, 1–10: `__` *(also log it on the home page table)*

## Sub-page: Day 2 — Quiet the Noise

**[Callout]** ⏱️ Set a 7-minute timer. Dump everything. Messy is correct.

**[Toggle: "Brain Dump"]** — one big empty text area.

**[Toggle: "Sort it (3 minutes)"]**
1. **Bold** the three things above that are actually mine to solve this week.
2. One small action I can take on one of them tomorrow:
3. One thing I'm officially putting down (not my problem / not this week / not in my control):

**[Callout, small]** 💡 Loved this exercise? This is exactly what The WHY Method turns into a daily 10-minute system.

## Sub-page: Day 3 — Plug the Energy Leaks

**[Toggle: "The Energy Leak List"]**
1. My top five energy leaks (people, habits, tasks, apps — be specific):
2. The one leak that costs me the most every week:
3. The ONE boundary I will hold this week (what, with whom, starting when):
4. What I'm afraid will happen if I say no — vs. what will most likely actually happen:

**[Callout: "Borrow these scripts"]**
- At work: "I can take this on, but something else on my plate will need to move — which should it be?"
- The soft no: "I won't be able to do this justice right now, so I'll pass."
- The delay: "Let me check my week and get back to you tomorrow."
